# Pharmacy Inventory

Effective inventory management ensures the hospital never runs out of critical life-saving drugs while minimizing wastage due to expired medicines.

## Inventory Management

![Pharmacy Inventory](../assets/pharmacy-inventory.png)

### Key Features

- **Batch Tracking**: Every medicine entry requires a batch number, manufacturer date, and expiry date. This allows the system to enforce "First Expire, First Out" (FEFO) rules during billing.
- **Stock Alerts**: Medicines that fall below their configured `Min Stock Level` are highlighted, prompting the pharmacist to generate a new purchase order.
- **Expiry Alerts**: A dedicated section highlights medicines that are expiring within the next 30, 60, or 90 days.
- **GST Configuration**: When adding new medicine stock, the pharmacist assigns the appropriate HSN code and GST rate, ensuring full compliance with Indian tax laws.
