# Financial Reports & Profit/Loss

The Finance module provides detailed financial analytics to help the hospital administration understand their bottom line.

## Profit & Loss Statements

![Finance Reports](../assets/finance-reports.png)

### Key Reports

- **Hospital P&L**: A comprehensive Profit & Loss statement showing total income versus total expenses (salaries, inventory purchases, maintenance).
- **Departmental Revenue**: Break down income by department to see which specialties are generating the most revenue.
- **Doctor Payouts**: Automatically calculate revenue shares for visiting consultant doctors based on their consultation fees and performed surgeries.
- **Operational Expenses**: Track daily running costs, including utility bills, biomedical waste disposal fees, and marketing expenses.
