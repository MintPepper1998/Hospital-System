# Front Desk & Patient Registration

The Reception Portal is optimized for speed and accuracy, allowing front desk staff to manage heavy patient footfalls efficiently.

## Patient Registration

![Reception Dashboard](../assets/reception-dashboard.png)

When a new patient arrives, the receptionist captures their essential details:
- **Demographics**: Name, Age, Gender, Blood Group.
- **Contact**: Phone number and Emergency Contact.
- **Unique ID Generation**: The system instantly generates a unique Patient ID (e.g., `PAT-0123`) and prints a barcode registration card.

## Appointment Booking

![Appointment Booking](../assets/reception-appointments.png)

### Features
- **Doctor Schedules**: View the real-time availability of all doctors across different departments.
- **Time Slots**: Book 15-minute or 30-minute consultation slots.
- **Instant Billing**: Generate the initial OPD consultation bill and collect the fee immediately at the time of booking.
